Quick Answers

Frequently Asked

Where do I park?

Free general parking is available throughout Central City Park. Premium parking ($10) is available adjacent to the main gate, first-come first-served.

Is the stadium ADA accessible?

Yes. Wheelchair-accessible seating, restrooms, and parking are available throughout the venue. Email accessibility@maconbacon.com to arrange accommodations.

Can I bring outside food?

Outside food and drinks are not permitted, with the exception of a single sealed water bottle (under 20 oz) per guest.

What if it rains?

Tickets to officially canceled games are converted to a credit good for any 2026 home game (subject to availability). We don't refund tickets to delayed or weather-affected games.

Can I book Kevin Bacon for a private event?

Absolutely. Kevin appears at birthdays, weddings, school events, and corporate functions. Book at least 4 weeks ahead. Pricing starts at $250.

Do you host non-baseball events?

Yes! Luther Williams Field hosts concerts, fundraisers, weddings and corporate events year-round. Email events@maconbacon.com for venue info.
