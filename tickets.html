FAQ

Ticketing Questions

How do I receive my tickets?

All tickets are mobile by default and delivered via the Macon Bacon app or email. Print-at-home is also available at checkout.

What's your refund policy?

Tickets to rainout games are automatically converted to a credit good for any 2026 home game (subject to availability).

Can I bring a bag into the stadium?

Soft-sided bags 14"x14"x6" or smaller are permitted. All bags are subject to search at the gate.

Are kids free?

Children 2 and under are free if they sit on a parent's lap. Sundays are Kids Eat Free with any paid adult ticket.
