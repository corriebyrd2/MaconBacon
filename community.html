Featured Story

A Summer of Smiles

In partnership with Beverly Knight Olson Children's Hospital, our players spent every Tuesday morning visiting young patients — bringing autographed gear, video games, and a much-needed laugh.

Player Miguel Ramirez (LSU) spearheaded the program, raising over $12,000 for the pediatric oncology unit through his "Sizzle Strikeouts" challenge: every K he recorded, $50 was donated.

"We get to play baseball every day. These kids deserve to feel that joy too," said Ramirez. "It's the most meaningful thing we do all summer."
